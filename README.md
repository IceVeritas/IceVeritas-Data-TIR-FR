# IceVeritas-Data-TIR-FR

### OSINT Research · Heavy Goods Vehicle Safety · French Mediterranean Motorway Corridor

## 1. Project Overview

IceVeritas-Data-TIR-FR is an independent research and documentation project focused on heavy goods vehicle (HGV) driver safety, truck parking security and motorway service areas along the French Mediterranean Corridor.

The research covers the A7, A8 and A9 motorways and examines documented security incidents, infrastructure conditions, operational practices and the responsibilities of motorway concessionaires.

Particular attention is given to:

* Autoroutes du Sud de la France (ASF).
* ESCOTA.
* VINCI Autoroutes, the concession group to which ASF and ESCOTA belong.

## 2. Geographic Scope

The research focuses on the French Mediterranean motorway corridor:

* A7 — Autoroute du Soleil.
* A8 — La Provençale.
* A9 — La Languedocienne / La Catalane.

The geographical scope includes motorway service areas, rest areas and parking facilities used by professional drivers.

## 3. Research Objectives

The project aims to:

* Document publicly reported thefts and security incidents affecting HGV drivers.
* Examine security conditions in truck parking facilities.
* Assess publicly documented infrastructure and security measures.
* Analyse concession contracts and relevant legal obligations.
* Document communications and responses from motorway concessionaires and public institutions.
* Identify recurring patterns and areas requiring further investigation.

## 4. Research Methodology

The project uses Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) methods and publicly accessible information.

Sources may include:

* Public reviews and user-generated reports.
* Official communications and institutional publications.
* Concession contracts and regulatory documents.
* Publicly available photographs and other documentary material.
* Structured datasets and independently documented observations.

Information is classified according to its source, date, geographical reference and verification status.

A reported incident is not automatically treated as an independently verified fact.

## 5. Data Integrity and Documentation

Where applicable, the project uses:

* Unique evidence identifiers.
* Original-file preservation.
* SHA-256 cryptographic hashes.
* Evidence manifests.
* Separate records for original material and analytical derivatives.
* Documented collection and processing procedures.

Cryptographic hashes support file-integrity verification but do not, by themselves, establish the authenticity or truthfulness of the underlying information.

## 6. Repository Structure

The repository is intended to organise:

* Datasets and incident records.
* Motorway service-area documentation.
* Institutional correspondence.
* Concession contracts and legal framework.
* Research methodology.
* Evidence-integrity records.
* Analytical reports.

## 7. Legal and Ethical Considerations

The project relies on publicly accessible information and aims to respect applicable data-protection, privacy and intellectual-property requirements.

Personal data, sensitive information and materials whose redistribution is not authorised are excluded from public publication or appropriately protected.

The project does not seek to attribute criminal responsibility to individuals or organisations without sufficient evidence.

## 8. Independence and Scope

IceVeritas is an independent documentation and research initiative.

The inclusion of an organisation, concessionaire, location or incident in this repository does not, by itself, imply wrongdoing.

Findings and assessments are based on the documentary material available and are subject to revision when additional evidence becomes available.

## 9. Project Updates

Datasets, documentation and research findings may be updated as new information is collected, verified and processed.

Each significant update should be documented to preserve the research history and traceability.
